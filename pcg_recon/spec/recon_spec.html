Bank Reconciliation System

One engine that reconciles every bank and custodian cash account of Page Asset Management Limited and Page Capital Limited against the books, daily for client and fund money and monthly for operating accounts, with maker-checker sign-off and an audit trail an examiner can walk.

Version 0.1 draftDate 29 Sep 2026Owner Group CEO/CIOBuild pcg-recon v0.1 (working)

01Scope

Every cash account the group controls, across both licensed entities. The account type drives cadence, tolerance and escalation.

EntityAccount typeExamplesCadenceTolerance
PAMOperatingFees, payroll, vendorsMonthlyNil
PAMClient money client moneySubscription / redemption collectionDailyNil, no tolerance matches
PAMFund custody client moneyFund cash at custodian / trusteeDailyNil, no tolerance matches
PCLOperatingOpex, funding linesMonthlyNil

Out of scope for v1: securities position reconciliation (units at custodian vs fund register), FX revaluation, intercompany netting. The data model leaves room for all three.

02Architecture

Five layers. Each source is normalised into one transaction shape before any matching happens, so adding a bank means adding a column mapping, not code.

03Ingestion

SourceHow it is readIntegrity check
Bank CSV / XLSXProfile of header synonyms; header row located automatically below letterhead; split Dr/Cr or signed + DR/CR flag; newest-first exports re-orderedEvery running balance recomputed
PDF statementText layer parsed line by line; sign taken from balance movement, not column position; wrapped narrations re-joinedBalance movement vs printed amount; stated closing
MT940:60F: / :61: / :86: / :62F:, reversal marks RC/RDOpening + lines = closing
Custodian cashSame tabular reader, custodian profileRunning cash balance
Accounting exportsSage, QuickBooks, Odoo, Excel cashbook profiles; account filter for multi-account journalsRunning balance where present; opening vs prior closing
Postgres ledgerRead-only SQL on posted journal lines for the bank GL codeOpening = sum of posted lines before period

All money is held as integer kobo. Multi-file downloads are chained (closing of file n must equal opening of file n+1) and overlapping rows de-duplicated.

04Matching passes

Passes run from most to least certain. Each only sees what earlier passes left. A match at or above 0.90 confidence is accepted automatically; anything below goes to a reviewer and the items stay open on the statement until approved.

PassRuleTypical caseConfidence
DReviewer decisions from earlier runs: approve, manual pair, rejectAnything a human settled1.00
P0Equal and opposite items on one side, within 7 days, with reversal wording or shared referenceFailed NIP transfer and its reversal0.97
P1Shared strong reference and equal amountNIP session ID, cheque number, PV / subscription code0.95–1.00
P5Carried bank-only items of one category cleared by a later catch-up journalLast month's EMTL booked as one JV0.80–0.95
P2Equal amount inside the date window; ranked by date gap and narration similarity. Ambiguous or small unsupported pairs are capped below thresholdVendor payment dated a day apart0.80–0.96
P3One-to-many and many-to-one exact sums (up to 6 items). Unique solution plus shared reference or narration needed for autoBulk payroll batch; tranched receipt0.70–0.95
P4Narration similarity outside the window; optional tolerance, never on client moneyLate-posted entry≤ 0.85, always review

Cheques get their own window: book date up to 45 days before bank date, never materially after.

05Reconciliation statement

Standard two-sided form. Every adjusting line in the Excel pack is a live SUMIFS over the open-items sheet, so each figure traces to its lines. Example below is the October run of the client collections account from the test data.

Balance per bank statement535,899,650.00
Add: deposits in transit–
Less: unpresented cheques / outstanding payments–
Adjusted bank balance535,899,650.00
Balance per cashbook520,904,400.00
Add: bank credits not in book15,000,000.00
Less: bank debits not in book (EMTL, SMS, VAT)(4,750.00)
Adjusted book balance535,899,650.00
Unreconciled difference0.00

The ₦15m of credits not in book are two unidentified receipts into a client account. The statement balances, but the run is marked RECONCILED-WITH-EXCEPTIONS and the items go to AML review. A zero difference is necessary, not sufficient.

07Controls

ControlHow it is enforced
Maker-checkerPreparer cannot sign their own run. Unreconciled runs need a written explanation to sign.
Segregation of dutiesReconciler has read-only ledger access. Journals leave as drafts through the normal approval flow.
Completeness of statementsRunning-balance chain on every line; file chain across downloads; stated vs computed closing.
Period integrityStatement and ledger openings must equal the prior run's closings. A mismatch signals back-dated postings.
Rule change controlHash of rules.yaml and every input file stamped on each run.
Tamper evidenceSHA-256 chained audit log of runs, decisions and sign-offs; verify-audit reports the first broken entry.
Client money protectionNo tolerance matches, daily cadence, 2-day escalation, unidentified credits to AML.
Human judgement keptLow-confidence matches never reduce open items until approved.

10Data model

TableHolds
runsOne row per account per period: balances, difference, status, maker, checker, rule and input hashes
open_itemsUnmatched lines at run close; the pool carried into the next run
decisionsReviewer approvals, rejections and manual pairings, keyed by line IDs
audit_logHash-chained record of every run, decision and sign-off

v0.1 stores these in SQLite. The schema is plain SQL and moves into the group PostgreSQL (AWS af-south-1) unchanged, next to the ledger.

11What v0.1 does today

The engine is built and tested on two months of synthetic data for five example accounts, one per input format, with every reconciling item a real month produces planted in it.

CheckResult
Closing balances parsed vs known truth (10 runs, 5 formats)10 / 10 exact
Unreconciled differenceNil on all runs
September open items = true reconciling itemsExact
October: carried items cleared, only planted unknown credits remainExact
Randomised stress test, 30 datasets29 clean 1 correctly sent to review
Deleted statement line detectedContinuity alert raised
Self sign-off; tampered audit entryBoth refused / detected
Excel pack formulas139 formulas, 0 errors

The one review case in the stress test was two ₦9.8m receipts that could each clear the same item. The engine did not guess.

Synthetic data proves the logic, not the bank layouts. Each real bank export needs its column profile confirmed against one live download and one month already reconciled by hand before go-live.

13Decisions needed

  1. Bank and custodian list. Every account per entity, with GL codes, so the registry can be filled in.
  2. Ledger of record per entity. Which accounting package each entity and fund uses today, and when the Postgres ledger takes over.
  3. Thresholds. Confirm ₦50m large-item, 2-day client-money and 180-day stale-cheque defaults.
  4. Named roles. Makers, checkers and the MLRO contact for the AML route.
  5. Bank feeds. Which banks will provide MT940 or SFTP drops, so portal downloads can be retired.
  6. Suspense policy. Whether unidentified client-money receipts may ever be posted to suspense, or must be held until identified or returned.
